# Registering and signing in on the HitnScore web app

Use this guide to create a Personal account, register interest for a Club account, accept an organisation invitation, and sign in at [app.hitnscore.com](https://app.hitnscore.com/).

## Before you begin

You will need:

- access to your email inbox;
- an internet connection; and
- a password of at least eight characters when you are asked to create or reset one.

Your email address is also your HitnScore username.

## Register for a Personal account

Personal accounts are created immediately and do not need approval from the HitnScore team.

1. Open the [HitnScore sign-in page](https://app.hitnscore.com/login).
2. Select **Want In**.
3. Enter your first name, surname and email address.
4. Choose **Personal use**.
5. Complete the human-check question.
6. Select **Create Personal Account**.
7. Check your inbox for the HitnScore account email.
8. Open the secure link in the email. This verifies your email address and lets you choose your password.
9. Return to the sign-in page and sign in using your email address and new password.

The email step must be completed before the account can be used. Check your spam or junk folder if the message does not appear within a few minutes.

## Register interest for a Club account

Club accounts are set up with the HitnScore team and are not created automatically from the form.

1. Open the [Want In form](https://app.hitnscore.com/login?interest=1#want-in).
2. Enter your first name, surname and email address.
3. Choose **Club use**.
4. Enter the club name and complete the human-check question.
5. Select **Register Club Interest**.

HitnScore will record the enquiry and contact you about the club setup. Submitting the form does not immediately provide access to a club workspace.

Once the club has been created, its users are added by invitation:

1. The user receives a HitnScore organisation invitation email.
2. The user selects **Approve access** in the email.
3. Their club membership becomes active.
4. They can sign in with the email address and password associated with their HitnScore account.

An existing HitnScore user keeps their existing password when they join another organisation. A new club user receives login details through the club onboarding process. If the user does not know their password, they can request a password reset.

## When is approval required?

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| New Personal account | No staff approval is required. The user verifies their email and sets a password. |
| Club account enquiry | The HitnScore team reviews the enquiry and contacts the requester before creating the club workspace. |
| User invited to a club | The membership remains pending until the user accepts the emailed approval link. A root administrator can also approve it manually. |
| Existing user joins another club | The user accepts the new invitation, then chooses the required organisation after signing in. |

## Sign in on the web

1. Open [app.hitnscore.com/login](https://app.hitnscore.com/login).
2. Enter your email address in **Username**.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign In**.

If your email has one approved membership, HitnScore takes you directly to its dashboard.

If your email belongs to several approved Personal or Club accounts, a **Choose Organisation** screen appears. Select the account you want to use. You can later switch between available associations from Settings.

## If HitnScore says you are already signed in

HitnScore permits one active web session for an account at a time. If the account is already open in another browser session, you can:

- select **Cancel** and continue using the existing session; or
- select **Log Out Other Web Session** to close it and continue in the current browser.

This does not sign the same account out of the iPhone app because web and mobile sessions are managed separately.

## If access is still pending

The message **Your organisation access is pending email approval** means that the club invitation has not been approved yet.

Check the invitation email and open the full approval link. If the link is missing or no longer works, contact the club administrator or HitnScore support so the invitation can be resent or approved manually.

## Forgotten password

1. Select **Need help?** from the sign-in page.
2. Open **Reset Password**.
3. Enter the email address used for the account.
4. Open the reset link sent by email.
5. Choose a new password and return to sign in.

For security, the reset screen displays the same confirmation whether or not an email address is registered.

## Common sign-in problems

- **Invalid credentials:** check that the email address is correct and that you are using the latest password.
- **Pending approval:** accept the organisation invitation or ask an administrator to approve it.
- **No email received:** check spam and junk folders, then request another password reset or invitation.
- **Several organisations shown:** select the Personal or Club account you want to use for this session.
- **Already signed in:** choose whether to keep the other web session or replace it.
