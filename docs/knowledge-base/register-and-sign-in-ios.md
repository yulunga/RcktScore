# Registering and signing in on the HitnScore iPhone app

Use this guide to register, complete any required approval, sign in, choose between linked accounts, and optionally enable Face ID or Touch ID in the HitnScore iPhone app.

## Before you begin

You will need:

- the HitnScore app installed on your iPhone;
- an internet connection for registration and normal password sign-in; and
- access to your email inbox.

Your email address is also your HitnScore username.

## Register for a Personal account

Personal accounts are created immediately and do not require approval from the HitnScore team.

1. Open the HitnScore app.
2. On the sign-in screen, select **Want In**.
3. Choose **Personal**.
4. Enter your first name, surname and email address.
5. Complete the human-check question.
6. Select **Create Personal Account**.
7. Wait for the confirmation screen, then check your email.
8. Open the secure link in the email to verify your address and set your password.
9. Return to the app and sign in with your email address and new password.

The account cannot be used until the email link has been completed. Check spam or junk folders if the message does not appear.

## Register interest for a Club account

Club accounts require setup by the HitnScore team.

1. Open the app and select **Want In**.
2. Choose **Club**.
3. Enter your first name, surname, email address and club name.
4. Complete the human-check question.
5. Select **Register Club Interest**.

The confirmation screen explains that the HitnScore team will review the enquiry and contact you about the next steps. The enquiry itself does not create an active club login.

When the club workspace is ready, users are invited separately:

1. Open the HitnScore invitation email.
2. Select **Approve access**.
3. Return to the app and sign in.

Existing HitnScore users continue using their existing password. New club users receive login details through the club onboarding process. A root administrator can approve a pending membership manually when required.

## When is approval required?

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| New Personal account | No staff approval is required. Verify the email address and set a password. |
| Club account enquiry | The HitnScore team reviews the request and contacts the requester before creating the club workspace. |
| User invited to a club | The membership remains pending until the emailed approval link is accepted or a root administrator approves it. |
| Existing user joins another organisation | Accept the invitation, then choose the required account when signing in. |

## Sign in with your email address and password

1. Open the HitnScore app while connected to the internet.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Use the eye button if you need to show or hide the password while entering it.
4. Select **Sign In**.

If the account has one approved membership, the app opens its dashboard.

If the email is linked to multiple approved Personal or Club accounts, the app displays **Choose Organisation**. Each choice shows the organisation and plan. Select the account you want to open. You can later switch between available associations from Settings.

## If the app says you are already signed in

HitnScore permits one active mobile session for an account at a time. If the account is active on another iPhone or iPad session, the app asks whether to replace it.

- Cancel to leave the existing mobile session active.
- Confirm the replacement to sign out the other mobile session and continue on this device.

A web session is separate and can remain signed in at the same time.

## Face ID or Touch ID sign-in

After signing in normally, you can enable biometric session unlock from Settings on that device.

When enabled, the sign-in screen offers **Sign in with Face ID** or **Sign in with Touch ID**. This reopens the saved, device-bound session; it does not replace your HitnScore password.

Biometric unlock works only while the saved server session remains valid. The app discards an expired session and asks you to sign in with your email address and password again. A previously saved valid session can also be reopened biometrically when the device is offline.

## If access is still pending

The message **Your organisation access is pending email approval** means the club invitation has not yet been approved.

Open the invitation email and select the full approval link. If it is missing or no longer works, ask the club administrator or HitnScore support to resend the invitation or arrange manual approval.

## Forgotten password

1. Select **Need Help** on the app’s sign-in screen.
2. Choose the password-reset option.
3. Enter the email address used for the account.
4. Open the reset email and choose a new password.
5. Return to the app and sign in.

For security, the request confirmation does not reveal whether an email address is registered.

## Common sign-in problems

- **Offline:** connect to the internet for password sign-in. Biometric unlock is available offline only for an unexpired session previously saved on that device.
- **Invalid credentials:** check the email address and password, or request a password reset.
- **Pending approval:** accept the invitation or contact the club administrator.
- **Several accounts shown:** select the correct Personal or Club organisation.
- **Already signed in:** decide whether to retain or replace the existing mobile session.
- **Face ID or Touch ID unavailable:** sign in normally, then check the biometric option in Settings.
