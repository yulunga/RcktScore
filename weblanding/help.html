Web client guide

Registering and signing in on the web

Create a Personal account, register interest for a Club account, complete approval and sign in at app.hitnscore.com.

Register for a Personal account

Personal accounts are created immediately and do not need approval from the HitNScore team.

  1. Open the HitNScore sign-in page and select Want In.
  2. Enter your first name, surname and email address.
  3. Choose Personal use, complete the human check and select Create Personal Account.
  4. Open the account email, verify your address and choose a password.
  5. Return to HitNScore and sign in using your email address and new password.

The email step activates access. Check your spam or junk folder if the message does not appear.

Register interest for a Club account

Club accounts are set up with the HitNScore team and are not created automatically from the form.

  1. Open Want In and choose Club use.
  2. Enter your contact details and club name, then complete the human check.
  3. Select Register Club Interest.
  4. Wait for the HitNScore team to review the enquiry and contact you about setup.

When the club is ready, each user receives an organisation invitation. Select Approve access in the email before signing in. Existing users keep their current password; new users receive login details through club onboarding.

When is approval required?

Personal accountNo staff approval. Verify your email and set a password.
Club enquiryThe HitNScore team reviews the request before creating a club workspace.
Club invitationAccept the emailed approval link, or ask a root administrator to approve it.
Another organisationAccept the invitation, then choose the account when signing in.

Sign in and choose an organisation

  1. Enter your account email in Username.
  2. Enter your password and select Sign In.
  3. If several approved accounts are linked to the email, choose the required organisation.

If another web session is active, HitNScore asks whether to keep it or sign it out and continue in the current browser. Web and iPhone sessions are managed separately.

Pending access or forgotten password

A pending-approval message means the organisation invitation has not been accepted. Open the invitation link or ask the club administrator for manual approval.

For a forgotten password, open Reset Password, enter the account email and follow the secure link sent to your inbox.

iPhone app guide

Registering and signing in on iPhone

Register in the HitNScore app, complete any required approval, choose between linked accounts and optionally use Face ID or Touch ID.

Register for a Personal account

Personal accounts are created immediately and do not require staff approval.

  1. Open the app and select Want In.
  2. Choose Personal and enter your first name, surname and email address.
  3. Complete the human check and select Create Personal Account.
  4. Open the verification email, confirm your address and set your password.
  5. Return to the app and sign in with your email address and new password.

Register interest for a Club account

  1. Select Want In, then choose Club.
  2. Enter your contact details and club name.
  3. Complete the human check and select Register Club Interest.
  4. Wait for the HitNScore team to review the enquiry and contact you.

Club users receive a separate organisation invitation. Select Approve access in that email before signing in. A root administrator can approve a pending membership manually when required.

When is approval required?

Personal accountNo staff approval. Verify your email and set a password.
Club enquiryThe HitNScore team reviews the request before creating a club workspace.
Club invitationAccept the emailed approval link, or ask a root administrator to approve it.
Another organisationAccept the invitation, then choose the account when signing in.

Sign in on iPhone

  1. Connect the device to the internet.
  2. Enter your email and password. Use the eye button to show or hide the password.
  3. Select Sign In.
  4. If several accounts are linked, choose the required organisation and plan.

If another mobile session is active, cancel to keep it or confirm the replacement to continue on this device. A web session can remain active at the same time.

Face ID or Touch ID

After a normal sign-in, biometric session unlock can be enabled in Settings. It reopens the saved, device-bound session and does not replace your password. It works only while that saved server session remains valid.

A previously saved valid session can be reopened biometrically while offline. New registrations and password sign-in still require an internet connection.

Pending access or forgotten password

If access is pending, accept the organisation invitation or ask the club administrator for help. For a forgotten password, select Need Help on the app sign-in screen and follow the password-reset steps.
