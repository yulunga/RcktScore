Useful answers

Before you start scoring.

The practical details players and clubs ask about most often.

Is Personal really free?

Yes. Create a personal account and use core scoring without paying. Your latest three completed matches remain readable.

What happens to older matches?

They remain stored. Personal Plus expands readable history to the latest 100 completed matches across signed-in devices.

Which sports work today?

Squash, racketball, tennis and doubles Padel are available, subject to the sports enabled for your account or club.

Can I score without a connection?

A previously opened active match can continue offline on iPhone and queued actions replay when the connection returns. Other areas still require connectivity.

How does my club join?

Register your interest, choose club use and provide your club details. We will follow up about onboarding and the most suitable plan.

What is different about Club Pro?

Club Pro is an enquiry-led higher tier. We discuss your scale and future requirements, with final package details confirmed during onboarding.
