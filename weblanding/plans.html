Before you choose

A few useful answers.

Is Personal really free?

Yes. You can create a personal account and use core scoring without paying. The latest three completed matches remain readable in the Free plan.

What happens to older matches?

They remain stored. Personal Plus expands readable history to the latest 100 completed matches across signed-in devices.

Which sports work today?

Squash, racketball and tennis are implemented. Availability also depends on which sports are enabled for your account or club.

Can I score without a connection?

On iPhone, a previously opened active match can continue offline and queued actions replay when connectivity returns. Other areas still require a connection.

How does my club join?

Register your interest, select “Club use,” and provide your club name. Our team will follow up about setup and the plan that fits.

What is different about Club Pro?

Club Pro is currently an enquiry-led higher-tier package. We discuss your scale and future requirements without promising premium limits or features before they are ready.
